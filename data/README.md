# Data dictionary

## Current comparison (v0.10.0)

`current_ranking.csv` is the current unified register: **11 unranked hypotheses, 26 total entries**. v0.10 withdraws every former 1/2/3 tier for the 11 new candidates. `rank_tier` is blank; `v09_recurrence_tier` and `v08_specificity_tier` preserve the former classifications as history, not current scores. The current register is manually reasoned, not generated by `tools/screen.py`.

The current question is whether a target-specific observation favors extra physical HBM over an ordinary period-compatible configuration. Product resemblance, repeated commercial purpose and a larger sibling motivate searching; they do not by themselves discriminate a hidden-capacity implementation. A normal catalog countermodel is not a verified target BOM, and missing target inputs are not positive evidence for hidden memory. See [diagnostic inference](../docs/diagnostic-inference.md) and the [current comparison](../docs/current-ranking.md).

The 11 hypotheses comprise three `information_gap`, one `topology_gap` and seven `ordinary_fit` entries. These are evidence classes, **not ordered success tiers**. All 11 retain `not_established` for physical excess, healthy excess and access. The other 15 references, comparators and watches retain their existing categories. H100 NVL remains a geometry reference: a minimum connected-stack count is not a physical-capacity measurement.

Only CMP 100-200 has `investigation_priority=first_documentary_check`. This selects a useful next documentary question: obtain same-unit width **and** memory rate or well-conditioned external-memory throughput and compare them with the documented low-density-part countermodel. Width alone would not distinguish ordinary 2 GB stacks from capped denser stacks. The label is neither a probability nor a hardware-purchase recommendation; blank priority cells mean no comparative priority is assigned.

## Period HBM part constraints

`hbm_part_constraints.csv` contains **eight documentary part/capability records** used to construct ordinary alternatives. They are not observations of candidate boards and do not form an exhaustive worldwide parts catalog. The fastest grade shown in a source is a listed grade, not a proven global or candidate-specific maximum. A missing low-density grade is not proof that none existed.

Dates preserve source precision (year, month or day). Capacity stays in the source's GB class without a silent GB/GiB conversion. Computed bandwidth such as 409.6 GB/s remains distinct from a source's rounded 410 GB/s. No row proves that a candidate manufacturer qualified, purchased or populated that part. See [diagnostic inference](../docs/diagnostic-inference.md).

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id`, `manufacturer`, `memory_type` | Stable record ID, document's manufacturer and memory generation |
| `document_date`, `date_type` | Sourced date and what it dates, retaining original precision; not a candidate board's manufacture date |
| `capacity_gb_class` | Source's nominal GB-class capacity per memory package/stack; not a measured exact byte count |
| `native_data_lanes` | Documented native data-lane count per package/stack; not the candidate's active width |
| `pin_rate_gbps` | Listed per-pin transfer rate in decimal gigabits per second; not a GPU clock reading or universal speed ceiling |
| `peak_gb_s` | Listed or computed theoretical bandwidth in decimal gigabytes per second, with its origin in `peak_kind` |
| `peak_kind` | Distinguishes a source-stated peak, a rounded source value and a computed theoretical value; never a board benchmark |
| `availability_evidence` | What the document establishes about a listed part, announcement or production status; does not establish candidate procurement |
| `qualification_scope` | Limits on package/controller compatibility and target qualification; undocumented qualification remains undocumented |
| `notes` | Density, voltage, rate, package and rounding assumptions needed to interpret the row |
| `source_ids` | References to the shared source register; repeated data from one announcement are not independent evidence |

## Performance observation ledger

`performance_gap_observations.csv` (introduced in v0.9.1) separates source-reported measurements, device reports, tool-derived theoretical peaks and vendor peaks. Date type distinguishes publication, document month and review date; review date is not experiment date. Capacity labels are not exact bytes. `value_min`/`value_max` represent reported vector-width ranges or a single value duplicated, not uncertainty intervals. Unknown batch, clocks and dates remain unknown. See [performance-gap screen](../docs/performance-gap.md).

This ledger supports reproducing nominal comparisons, not reinstating the withdrawn v0.9 tiers. A high bandwidth/capacity ratio is a descriptive screen; an ordinary period-compatible density/rate combination may produce it. The known CMP 170HX outcome is retrospective calibration, not an independent prospective validation of a detector.

## Capacity-control precedent ledger

`capacity_precedents.csv` records four selected historical reference cases, not a random sample or a rate denominator. Its purpose is to distinguish recurring outcomes from a proven common cause and to keep architecture, manufacture and report dates separate. Dates retain their supported precision; blanks mean unknown. Read each `scope` before transferring a precedent to a new candidate. The selected cases supply hypothesis context, not a denominator or current ranking input. A vendor-hosted owner post remains an owner claim.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id`, `vendor`, `product`, `memory_type` | Stable case ID and exact product/memory scope; GDDR precedents are not HBM population evidence |
| `hardware_anchor_date` | Sourced architecture/product date, if available; not an individual board's manufacture date |
| `hardware_anchor_type`, `hardware_anchor_scope` | What that date actually dates and which architecture/product it covers |
| `board_manufacture_date` | Sourced date of the observed board's manufacture. All four rows are currently blank |
| `report_date`, `report_date_type` | The dated post/article or explicitly reported experiment date, with its meaning stated. An experiment date is not automatically publication date or first discovery |
| `reviewed_date` | This project's review date, not an event in the product's history |
| `reported_evidence` | Attributed observation/report and its coverage; not a measurement performed by this project |
| `scope` | Sample, model and provenance limitations, including mixed reports and non-independent replication |
| `reusable_pattern` | The limited result or productization pattern that motivates a new forecast |
| `cause_status` | Whether the manufacturing motivation is established; repeated outcomes alone do not establish common intent or supply causation |
| `source_ids` | References in the shared source register; multiple URLs may describe one event |

In particular, GP100's 2016 architecture date does not date manufacture of CMP 100-100. That row's 2026-11-02 value is the owner's reported two-card trial date. CMP 170HX has no asserted first-report date here. P104's 2018-12-02 owner post and the separate Gigabyte 4 GB specification do not identify the same physical unit.

## Historical datasets and observation ledgers

`historical_trigger_leads.csv` contains the v0.6.0 focused new/revisited leads, with rank 1–7, W for supply watch, C for ordinary/geometry comparator. This is a separate research-priority ordering, not a new ranking over all prior candidates. Capacity scenarios and comparison labels are not measured physical totals or recovery predictions.

`cross_vendor_leads.csv` is the v0.5.0 cross-vendor investigation order. `rank` is research priority for a concrete lead, not a recovery probability; `physical_evidence_status` separates documented physical surplus from unverified predictions, while `functional_excess_confidence` records the qualitative limitation. Conditional total labels are not measured or recoverable bytes. The older `hypotheses.csv` and `devices.csv` retain the v0.4.0 NVIDIA-centered forecast/configuration records. All such historical rank labels are superseded by the unranked v0.10 register.

`hypotheses.csv` is the main prediction register for v0.4.0: one unverified candidate per row, with ordinal `rank`, explicit `prediction`, conditional nominal **total** capacity scenario, required assumptions, strongest ordinary alternative, cohort hypothesis, and conditions for raising/lowering the rank. `confidence` is a qualitative assessment, not a probability. All five rows have `hypothesis_status=unverified_prediction`; physical excess, healthy excess and software access are separately `not_established`. A low-confidence first place is possible. A30/PG199 have fragile relative ordering. Source IDs justify the stated inputs, not the hypothetical total capacity. No invented hardware observations are stored here.

In `devices.csv`, `priority` now contains `prediction_rank_1` through `prediction_rank_5` for the corresponding candidates, or `control` / `reference`. These are display labels copied from `hypotheses.csv`, not a result of the screening formula. The previous P1/P2 labels described information-collection priorities; v0.4.0 replaces them with the explicit prediction ranking. Larger conditional capacities remain in `hypotheses.csv`, never in factual capacity or width columns.

`devices.csv` has one row per named reference configuration, not per sample. Empty cells mean unknown/not established, never zero. `nominal_capacity_gb` preserves advertised GB-class labels for approximate comparison; for owner reports with GiB labels it is a nominal class, not a conversion into decimal GB. `runtime_total_mib` is filled only where a source explicitly uses MiB; the hashcat report uses MB and is described in notes instead. `effective_bus_bits` is filled only for a directly sourced width (manufacturer or attributed external report); `peak_bandwidth_gbps` means decimal GB/s (bytes, not bits) from a vendor or explicitly labeled external peak specification. The script accepts `bus_status=vendor`, `owner_verified` or `community_report` for computation, leaving missing or unverified widths blank. `reference_density_gb` is a chosen stated comparator, not automatically the minimum compatible density. `reference_basis` records its rationale. The output's q ratio is a heuristic, not a physical estimate. Source IDs refer to `docs/sources.md`.

`market_observations.csv` has one row per listing snapshot/transaction, never equating a sold counter with individual transactions. `observed_utc` may be a date if exact time is unavailable (note precision); `source_updated_at` is separate. Unknown quantities and shipping stay empty. Preserve original currency. `price_type` is `ask` or `transaction_verified` or `transaction_unverified`. No market index is computed from the single seed observation.

`supply_events.csv` records dated, sourced historical context. `date_type` distinguishes announcement, earnings call, protocol event, licensing notice and fiscal period end. `event_date` preserves ISO precision: `2025-04` means the month only, not April 1. A month row cannot be ordered relative to events inside that month without more evidence. `inference_limit` specifies why each event is not proof of actual lot substitution. The table is not a supply quantity or HBM inventory time series.

`lot_observations.csv` is intentionally header-only until actual owner evidence is submitted. One row per anonymized device observation, with a stable sample ID reused for repeated observations; no full serial/UUID. `date_type` identifies the manufacturing stage or nonmanufacturing event, `date_start`/`date_end` are inclusive bounds (ISO dates where supported), and `date_precision` and `date_source` document provenance. Raw redacted codes are not presumed decodable. `cohort_id` is a sourced non-identifying group, not an inferred lot number. Unknowns stay blank. `hbm_density_bytes_per_stack` and `populated_stack_count` require physical evidence; never fill an exact count or density just from bus width. A conditional minimum count derived from a per-stack lane maximum belongs in the theory, not in this observation field. `runtime_total_mib` is driver telemetry, `validated_distinct_bytes` is experimentally verified coverage, not total physical capacity. `evidence_status` can be `unreviewed`, `source_checked`, `replicated`, or `contradicted` for the stated claim; retain negative and uncertain records. `acquisition_channel_category` must not identify the owner. See [lot protocol](../docs/lot-history.md).

Keep source claims and predictions in their respective fields. Public-source corrections and alternative theoretical explanations can update the hypothesis register without hardware ownership. For actual runtime samples, use issues and supply redacted logs with provenance and explicit coverage.
