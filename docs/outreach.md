## What respondents should find / リンク先で伝えること

- The [current ranking](current-ranking.md) is a reasoned set of hypotheses. Documented inactive memory is separated from unverified healthy-extra-memory predictions.
- [Participation](call-for-participants.md) starts with one source, fact or counterexample. [Quick evidence](https://github.com/Unjuno/hbm-anomaly-research/issues/new?template=quick-evidence.md) accepts unknown fields.
- Existing owners can supply part numbers, existing photographs or observations without buying or modifying hardware. People without hardware can supply documents and competing explanations.
- A nominal driver capacity is not a negative physical-presence test. A larger assembly is not proof of health or usability. Preserve that distinction when replying to reports.

「通常構成だった例」は、通常容量の画面だけで物理余剰を否定するという意味ではありません。報告の解釈は[参加案内](call-for-participants.ja.md)に従い、資料・部品構成・表示・動作を区別します。肯定的な結果だけを集めず、反例と不明点も残します。
