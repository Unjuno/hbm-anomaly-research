# Contribute evidence

[日本語](call-for-participants.ja.md) · [Candidate list](current-ranking.md)

Share specifications, existing logs, original memory part identifiers or production records for a candidate. Ordinary configurations and counterexamples are equally useful.

**[Open an issue](https://github.com/Unjuno/hbm-anomaly-research/issues/new?template=quick-evidence.md)**

Three things are enough to start:

1. Product and model.
2. What you observed (capacity, width, throughput, parts).
3. Source URL or existing logs/photos.

No purchase, disassembly or modification is required. Remove serial numbers and other identifying information.
