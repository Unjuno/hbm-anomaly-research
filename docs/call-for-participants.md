# Contribute evidence and competing predictions

[Current ranking](candidates.md) · [Ranking method](ranking-method.md) · [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

We are publishing explicit predictions about HBM-equipped GPUs that may contain additional healthy memory beyond the advertised capacity. The central work is to compare why a product looks suspicious with why an ordinary configuration could explain it.

**You do not need to own a GPU to contribute.** Useful contributions include:

- A primary specification, part-number mapping, archived product document or first-person report.
- A concrete alternative explanation for one of the five predictions.
- A manufacturing or lot mechanism with its compatibility and timing assumptions stated.
- A reason two candidates should change order, including the premise that changes.
- Comparable dated market evidence that distinguishes revisions, condition and asking versus transaction prices.

Use the **New candidate / prediction / source correction** template. State the forecast, its inputs, its necessary assumptions and its strongest counterargument. Label hypothetical capacity as a scenario. A source attached to a prediction does not mean the source makes that prediction.

## Optional observations from existing owners

If you already own a relevant accelerator, stock identification, ordinary low-density findings and negative results can improve future editions. See the [owner protocol](validation.md) and [study design](study-design.md) for interpreting hardware evidence. No purchase or modification is required to contribute. Keep personal identifiers and proprietary material out of public reports.
