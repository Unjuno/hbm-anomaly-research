# Call for independent GPU owners and hardware researchers

Do you already own a CMP 100-200, NVIDIA A30, DRIVE A100 (PG199), CMP 100-210, A30X, or another unusual HBM-equipped accelerator?

We are building an open register of discrepancies between advertised memory capacity, memory geometry and product lineage. Existing community reports suggest that selected mining accelerators can expose additional memory, but this does not establish the same result for other products or revisions.

We are looking for **read-only stock observations**, especially PCI and subsystem IDs, board part/revision numbers, reported capacity, memory clocks, and independently documented HBM part numbers, and credible manufacturing-date ranges and their provenance. We especially need early/transition/late production cohorts of the same SKU, including normal configurations. See [manufacturing cohorts](lot-history.md). No purchase, modification or risky experiment is required. Existing negative results and evidence of normal lower-density assemblies are just as valuable as positive results.

If you can contribute, open a Hardware observation or Replication result issue. See the [candidate register](candidates.md) and [validation protocol](validation.md). Redact personal and unique device identifiers and do not upload proprietary firmware or security-sensitive details.

Our goal is to test the hypothesis publicly, not to claim that any candidate is guaranteed to have extra memory or to encourage hardware speculation.
