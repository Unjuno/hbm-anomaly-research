# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-26

Adds a cross-vendor research-priority ranking and CSV, with Intel GPU Max 1100's documented physical excess (not established healthy or recoverable capacity), AMD MI50, RX Vega, BC-160, the RX 480 historical GDDR5 positive report and contemporary supply explanation, and separate non-GPU/ordinary comparisons. Retains v0.4.0 as a historical NVIDIA-only forecast. No new hardware measurements, calibrated probabilities or price adjustments.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-25

Refocuses the project on its intended deliverable: an explicitly speculative ranking of hidden healthy HBM hypotheses from public evidence and theory. Puts five ordered predictions at the top of both READMEs, adds Japanese candidate cards and a ranking-method document, and provides a separate hypothesis CSV. Each prediction states capacity scenarios, required assumptions, ordinary alternatives, cohort conditions and rank-reversal evidence. Reveals the weak A30/PG199 ordering and the judgment behind the A100 watchlist. Separates reported controls from new predictions; keeps owner validation as optional supporting material. Adds primary-source counterarguments: A30 compute recovery without memory recovery, CMP performance-versus-capacity distinctions, lower-density HBM2 possibilities, and ordinary AMD capacity/bandwidth examples. No hardware result, probability estimate or price-based ranking adjustment is added.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-25

Organizes the research into physical architecture, manufacturing economics, and evidential inference. Adds a Japanese research framework, a bounded-study design, and a study-proposal issue template. Separates physical excess, functional excess, production-cohort association, and supply causation. Corrects the bandwidth model to use a dimensionless data-lane count, excludes dedicated ECC/spare storage from payload capacity, and treats health and addressability as separate properties. Renames the theoretical reference density from a purported minimum to a stated comparator. Clarifies exact nominal baselines, device residency, reservations, alias checks, scope of failures and stopping rules. Updates primary-source links and the Japanese CMP announcement date. No new hardware measurements, validated price series, success probabilities or unlock claims.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-25

Adds a conditional supply-substitution economic model, dated primary-source manufacturing context, an empty per-device cohort ledger, date-type/provenance fields, stratified exploratory sampling, and explicit tests distinguishing BOM heterogeneity, temporal association, extra usable memory and causal supply evidence. No new hardware measurements or supplier-specific substitution claim.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

Initial desk-research release: theory, device register, source register, market schema, independent-owner protocol, issue templates, offline screening and unit tests. No original hardware measurements or calibrated market series. Unverified widths and larger capacities are not populated as factual values. Known counterexamples and negative results are retained.
