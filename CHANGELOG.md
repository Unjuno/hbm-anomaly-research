# Changelog

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-26

Separates installed, healthy and accessible capacity; moves Intel Max 1100 to a documented-disabled reference, H20 to supply watch, and Blackwell capacity discrepancies to specification/accounting watch. Retains prior rankings as labeled snapshots and corrects the historical Intel fourth-stack density wording without changing the old ordinal record.

Adds English/Japanese theory refinements: HBM2E address-density versus disabled-stack mechanisms, manufacturing-stage constraints, independent-source counting, publication-induced price feedback, and bounded counterexamples. Updates primary MI50/MIG sources, fixes the source-table break and the artificial day in the month-only AMD supply event. Rewrites the landing pages, adds brief bilingual evidence intake and candidate-specific requests, and prepares Japanese/English X drafts with compose links. Preparing a compose link does not post to X. No new hardware experiment, favorable lot, calibrated probability, verified price series or capacity recovery is claimed.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-25

Refocuses the project on its intended deliverable: an explicitly speculative ranking of hidden healthy HBM hypotheses from public evidence and theory. Puts five ordered predictions at the top of both READMEs, adds Japanese candidate cards and a ranking-method document, and provides a separate hypothesis CSV. Each prediction states capacity scenarios, required assumptions, ordinary alternatives, cohort conditions and rank-reversal evidence. Reveals the weak A30/PG199 ordering and the judgment behind the A100 watchlist. Separates reported controls from new predictions; keeps owner validation as optional supporting material. Adds primary-source counterarguments: A30 compute recovery without memory recovery, CMP performance-versus-capacity distinctions, lower-density HBM2 possibilities, and ordinary AMD capacity/bandwidth examples. No hardware result, probability estimate or price-based ranking adjustment is added.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-25

Organizes the research into physical architecture, manufacturing economics, and evidential inference. Adds a Japanese research framework, a bounded-study design, and a study-proposal issue template. Corrects the bandwidth model to use a dimensionless data-lane count, excludes dedicated ECC/spare storage from payload capacity, and treats health and addressability as separate properties. Renames the theoretical reference density from a purported minimum to a stated comparator. Clarifies exact nominal baselines, device residency, reservations, alias checks, scope of failures and stopping rules. Updates primary-source links and the Japanese CMP announcement date. No new hardware measurements, validated price series, success probabilities or unlock claims.
